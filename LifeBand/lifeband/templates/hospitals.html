Haversine Formula Implementation

Python Math

The Haversine formula determines the great-circle distance between two points on a sphere given their longitudes and latitudes:

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ₁)·cos(φ₂)·sin²(Δλ/2)
c = 2·atan2(√a, √(1−a))
d = R · c  (R = 6371.0 km)

Why not Euclidean distance? The Earth is an oblate spheroid. Planar Cartesian distance introduces up to 30% error at varying latitudes. Haversine provides <0.5% error.
