AI Architecture Explanation (For Faculty)

Why Deterministic Rules for First-Aid? In emergency medicine, clinical safety requires 100% predictable, hallucination-free outputs. Unconstrained probabilistic LLMs may recommend contraindicated medications (e.g. aspirin during hemorrhage).

Condition-Aware Reasoning: The Python engine dynamically parses the patient's medical profile tags, detects cross-sensitivities (e.g. NSAID contraindications, asthma positioning), and generates tailored protocols instantly.
