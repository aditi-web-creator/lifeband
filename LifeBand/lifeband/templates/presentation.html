Faculty Viva Defense Questions & Answers

Q&A Sheet
Q1: How does the Fall Detection algorithm work? (IoT Aspect)

Answer: It reads linear acceleration along three spatial orthogonal axes ($a_x, a_y, a_z$) from the device accelerometer via the DeviceMotion API. It computes the Euclidean vector magnitude $|a| = \sqrt{a_x^2 + a_y^2 + a_z^2}$. When $|a|$ spikes above a calibrated impact threshold (25.0 m/s²) followed by sudden immobility, an impact is inferred.

Q2: How does the blockchain hash chain protect medical records? (Blockchain Aspect)

Answer: Each record revision includes the SHA-256 hash of its predecessor. Because SHA-256 is collision-resistant and possesses the avalanche effect, if an unauthorized actor alters an allergy or blood group directly in SQLite, recomputing the hash produces a mismatch with the stored hash and breaks all subsequent links.

Q3: Why use the Haversine formula instead of Euclidean distance? (GPS / Math Aspect)

Answer: Euclidean planar distance ($d = \sqrt{\Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2}$) assumes a flat Cartesian plane. Because the Earth is spherical, lines of longitude converge at the poles. The spherical Haversine formula calculates the exact great-circle distance on a sphere of radius 6,371 km, ensuring high accuracy.

Q4: Why use deterministic Python rules for AI First-Aid instead of pure LLM prompts?

Answer: In emergency triage, hallucination or latency is dangerous. Deterministic clinical logic guarantees zero hallucination, strict contraindication filtering (e.g. preventing Aspirin administration to an allergic patient), and runs completely offline without requiring paid API keys or cloud dependencies.

Q5: What are the current limitations and future scope of LifeBand?

Answer: Currently, alerts are simulated within SQLite and local hash chains. Future enhancements include: (1) Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) smart band integration, (2) GSM/SMS hardware modules for cellular fail-safe alerts without internet, and (3) Smart Contract anchoring on Polygon/Ethereum for decentralized cross-hospital interoperability.
